Privacy Policy

Digital Twin, by Amphibia · amphibiatech.com

Digital Twin is an application built by Amphibia that helps a business reply to its own Instagram direct messages. This policy explains what it handles and why.

Who this covers

Two groups. The business that connects its Instagram professional account to Digital Twin, and the people who send that business a direct message.

What we access

When a business connects its account, Digital Twin uses Instagram's official API, with the permissions that business grants, to access:

  • The business account's username and Instagram user ID.
  • Direct message conversations belonging to that account: message text, the timestamp, and the Instagram-scoped ID and username of the person messaging.

We do not access followers, posts, media, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, or anything outside the connected account's direct messages.

Why

Solely to draft and send replies to that account's direct messages on the business's behalf. Message content is processed by an AI model to produce a suggested reply.

We do not sell this data, share it with advertisers, use it to build profiles of individuals, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Where it is stored

Message records are stored on infrastructure operated by Autessa, Amphibia's software platform provider, in the United States, in a database scoped to the individual business. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown to the AI model.

How long we keep it

Message records are kept while the business's account is connected, so the assistant has the conversation history it needs to reply sensibly. When a business disconnects, its stored messages are deleted within 30 days. See the deletion page below for requesting this sooner.

Your choices

  • A business can disconnect at any time by removing Digital Twin from its Instagram account settings, which immediately revokes our access.
  • Anyone who has messaged a business using Digital Twin may ask for their messages to be deleted from our records. See Data Deletion.

Children

Digital Twin is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly retain data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date below and notify connected businesses by email.
