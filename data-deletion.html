Deleting your data

Digital Twin, by Amphibia · amphibiatech.com

Disconnect the app

In Instagram: Settings and privacy → Apps and websites → Digital Twin → Remove. This revokes our access immediately. Stored message records are deleted within 30 days.

Delete sooner, or delete your messages

Email support@amphibiatech.com with the subject Data deletion and include the Instagram username involved.

This works both for a business that connected the app, and for anyone who has sent a message to a business using it. We will confirm once the records are deleted, normally within seven days.

What gets deleted

  • Stored direct message text and timestamps.
  • Instagram usernames and scoped user IDs we hold.
  • The stored access token for a connected account.

Messages still held by Instagram itself are not ours to delete; remove those in Instagram.
